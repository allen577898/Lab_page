通訊及信號訊息實驗室。Communications, Signal and Information Processing Lab.

CSIPL 研究無線通訊、信號處理與資訊理論，從編碼調變到干擾管理，讓每一個位元都傳得更遠、更可靠。CSIPL works on wireless communications, signal processing and information theory, from coding and modulation to interference management, so every bit travels farther and more reliably.

研究方向。Research.

從實體層到網路層的四條主線。Four directions, from the physical layer to the network.

編碼、調變與信號處理Coding, modulation and signal processing

MIMO 波束成形、混合式波束成形、通道估測、OTFS 與格狀編碼，提升頻譜效率與可靠度。MIMO and hybrid beamforming, channel estimation, OTFS and lattice coding for higher spectral efficiency and reliability.

干擾管理與資源分配Interference management and resource allocation

分散式大規模 MIMO、全雙工網路、D2D 與非正交多重接取的協同設計與最佳化。Distributed massive MIMO, full-duplex networks, D2D and non-orthogonal multiple access, designed and optimized jointly.

機器學習輔助通訊Machine learning for communications

以深度學習進行到達角估測、室內定位、碼本設計與通道估測，結合模型與資料驅動方法。Deep learning for DoA estimation, indoor localization, codebook design and channel estimation, combining model-based and data-driven methods.

雷達感測與物聯網Radar sensing and IoT

FMCW 雷達信號處理、無人機生命徵象偵測、M2M 與免授權上行傳輸的使用者偵測與通道估測。FMCW radar signal processing, UAV vital-sign detection, and user activity detection and channel estimation for M2M and grant-free uplink.

蘇炫榮Hsuan-Jung Su

國立臺灣大學電機工程學系教授Professor, Department of Electrical Engineering, National Taiwan University

國立交通大學電子工程學士，美國馬里蘭大學電機工程碩士與博士。曾任馬里蘭大學系統研究所博士後研究員、Lucent 貝爾實驗室研究員，2003 年加入台大電機系與電信所，2014 至 2015 年為普林斯頓大學訪問學者。研究領域涵蓋編碼調變、信號處理、干擾管理、資源分配，以及無線、認知無線電、M2M 與 D2D 網路的媒體存取協定。B.S. in Electronics Engineering from National Chiao Tung University; M.S. and Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Maryland, College Park. Postdoctoral researcher at Maryland's Institute for Systems Research and researcher at Bell Laboratories, Lucent Technologies, before joining NTU in 2003; Visiting Fellow at Princeton University in 2014 to 2015. Research covers coding, modulation, signal processing, interference management, resource allocation and MAC protocols for wireless, cognitive, M2M and D2D networks.
